## **🌐 High‑Level Project Summary (Refined)**

**Core Idea:**  
Build an **agentic AI personal assistant** that integrates with the professional’s existing tools (CRM, calendars, spreadsheets, messaging apps, payment apps, research sources) and acts as a unified command center. The assistant understands the professional’s **personal brand, workflow style, and service modality**, then automates the operational load that normally distracts them from client-facing work.

The assistant becomes a **multi‑tool orchestrator**:  
- It listens to what the professional types or says.  
- It fetches, updates, or writes data into connected apps.  
- It summarizes sessions, generates structured reports, manages scheduling, tracks tasks, and communicates with clients.  
- It creates daily plans and helps the professional stay organized without switching between apps.

The product is designed for **any 1:1 service provider**—life coaches, astrologers, therapists, home‑service professionals, independent sellers—who rely on client interaction and personalized guidance.

---

## **🎯 What the Assistant Actually Does**
- **Session Intelligence:**  
  Converts rough notes or audio recordings into structured summaries based on the professional’s predefined templates.  
  Pushes the final summary into CRM, spreadsheets, or other systems after approval.

- **Scheduling & Coordination:**  
  Checks availability across multiple calendars.  
  Books appointments, sends confirmations, and updates CRM or spreadsheets automatically.

- **Client Communication:**  
  Sends messages via email, WhatsApp, Instagram, or other channels based on the professional’s instructions.

- **Research Automation:**  
  Uses a personalized catalog of trusted websites to gather insights relevant to the professional’s modality.

- **Daily Planning:**  
  Generates a personalized day plan and task list based on meetings, deadlines, personal routines, and ongoing client needs.

- **Operational Tracking:**  
  Updates client records, payment status, appointment logs, and task lists in CRM or spreadsheets.

# **👤 Persona 1: Jane — The Professional Life Coach / Therapist**

### **Profile**
Jane is a trained life coach and therapist who works with clients 1:1. She uses a CRM to manage client data, scheduling, and progress tracking. She is tech‑comfortable but overwhelmed by administrative overhead.

### **Pain Points**
- During sessions, she struggles to take structured notes without losing presence.  
- She often records sessions or writes rough notes, but converting them into CRM‑ready summaries is time‑consuming.  
- Updating CRM, navigating multiple screens, and managing scheduling interrupts her flow.  
- Researching modalities (NLP, CBT, somatic work, astrology, etc.) requires visiting multiple websites manually.

### **Goals**
- Stay fully present during client sessions.  
- Reduce post‑session admin time.  
- Automate CRM updates and scheduling.  
- Maintain a consistent structure for client summaries.  
- Have a personalized research assistant.

### **Assistant Capabilities for Jane**
- Accepts scanned notes or audio recordings → produces structured summaries in her preferred template.  
- Uses both her notes and session recordings for accuracy.  
- After approval, updates CRM automatically and reports status back.  
- Books appointments by checking her personal calendar + CRM calendar.  
- Sends confirmations to clients.  
- Tracks her curated research websites and fetches relevant insights.  
- Generates a daily plan (work + personal life).

# **👤 Persona 2: Meera — The Less Tech‑Savvy Independent Service Provider**

### **Profile**
Meera runs a small home‑based business—cooking service, tailoring, clothing sales, cleaning services. She interacts with clients through WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. She tracks her business in simple tools like Google Sheets or Excel.

### **Pain Points**
- Scheduling is chaotic because client requests arrive through multiple social channels.  
- She manually checks her availability and responds to each client.  
- She tracks appointments and payments in spreadsheets, which is error‑prone.  
- She doesn’t have time or comfort to use complex CRM systems.

### **Goals**
- Quickly know her availability.  
- Respond to client requests without switching apps.  
- Keep her spreadsheet updated automatically.  
- Maintain a simple, reliable workflow.

### **Assistant Capabilities for Meera**
- Pulls her schedule from her calendar.  
- Reads client requests from WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook.  
- Checks availability and:  
  - If available → books appointment + sends confirmation message.  
  - If not → sends alternative availability.  
- Updates her Google Sheet with client name, request type, date, appointment status, and payment status.  
- Creates a simple daily plan and to‑do list.
